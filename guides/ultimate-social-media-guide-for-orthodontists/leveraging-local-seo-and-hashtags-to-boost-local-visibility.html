Chapter 9: Advanced Social Media Tips

Leveraging Local SEO and Hashtags to Boost Local Visibility

One of the biggest advantages of social media for orthodontic practices is the ability to target local patients. By effectively using local SEO (search engine optimization) tactics and location-based hashtags, you can increase your practice’s visibility within your community.

Use Local Hashtags

Hashtags play a critical role in getting your content discovered on platforms like Instagram and Twitter. Local hashtags can help attract users who are searching for services near them. Examples might include:

  • #YourCityOrthodontics (e.g., #LosAngelesOrthodontics)

  • #YourCityBraces

  • #InvisalignYourCity

  • #SmilesOfYourCity

    • In addition to these custom hashtags, research popular local hashtags related to your industry. This can include regional community events or seasonal hashtags.

    Tag Your Location

    Always tag your location in your posts, stories, and reels. This increases your chances of showing up in local search results and location-based feeds. On platforms like Instagram, this also allows local users to easily find your practice when browsing location-specific content.

    Optimize Your Profile for Local SEO

    Ensure your social media profiles are fully optimized for local search. This includes:

  • Listing your practice name, address, and phone number (NAP) clearly on platforms like Facebook, Instagram, LinkedIn, and Google My Business.

  • Mentioning your location in your bio or about section so that users can quickly identify that your practice is local.

  • Use local keywords in your posts. For example, incorporate your city or neighborhood in captions and descriptions.
