The Anatomy of a Consult Machine

What makes an orthodontic website convert

We've torn apart hundreds of orthodontic websites. The pretty ones and the profitable ones are rarely the same sites. Here's what separates the sites that book consults from the ones that just sit there.

Mobile-first, always

Parents research orthodontists from the school pickup line, not a desktop. If your site isn't designed thumb-first — big tap targets, instant menus, one-scroll booking — you're invisible where decisions actually happen.

Speed that scores

Every second of load time bleeds visitors — and Google ranks slow sites lower. Our builds are engineered for top PageSpeed marks, so patients (and search engines) never wait on you.

Consult CTAs everywhere

One clear ask — book the free consult — repeated on every screen, never more than a thumb-flick away. No "contact us" dead ends, no buried phone numbers, no guessing what to do next.

Financing transparency

"How much do braces cost?" is the question every parent is silently asking. Dedicated financing and pricing pages answer it honestly — which builds trust, captures high-intent searches, and pre-qualifies your consults.

Before & after galleries

Nothing sells orthodontics like finished smiles. Real transformation galleries do the convincing before you ever meet the patient — proof beats promises, every time.

Reviews, front and center

Your Google reviews are your best marketing asset — if anyone sees them. We integrate live review feeds into the site so five-star social proof greets every visitor, automatically.

Forms wired to book — not a black hole

This is where most orthodontic websites quietly die: a parent submits a form and hears back… Thursday. Every OrthoBoost form feeds straight into your booking flow and CRM — the lead lands with your front desk the instant it comes in, and the consult can be booked directly in your own practice software. No leads sitting in an inbox nobody checks.
