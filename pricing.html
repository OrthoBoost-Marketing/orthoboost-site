Pricing FAQ

Why are you so much cheaper than other agencies?+
Because we run lean on purpose. We've built streamlined internal systems for website builds, SEO, and campaign management, and we keep the team small and senior — no account-manager layer cake, no big-agency overhead baked into your bill. Same output, less machinery. Every agency will eventually work this way; we're just first.
How does the free $10,000 website work?+
Simple: start any Growth Plan — any location tier qualifies, not just the top one — complete the initial 3-month commitment, and the website is yours. Then you'd be buying the one piece that doesn't work alone. A website without traffic and follow-up is a very pretty brochure. You go month-to-month. No auto-renewal into another long contract, no cancellation fee. The 3-month runway exists because that's how long it takes to build the site, launch the campaigns, and let the data compound — after that, we keep your business by earning it every month.
