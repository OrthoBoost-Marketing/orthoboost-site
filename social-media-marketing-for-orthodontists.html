The Follow-Up Multiplier

Social leads go cold in minutes. Ours get a human.

Here's the dirty secret of orthodontic social media: most practices don't have an ads problem, they have a follow-up problem. A mom taps your ad between school pickup and dinner. If nobody reaches her fast, that lead is gone — she's back to scrolling, and your ad dollars went with her.

That's why every social campaign we run is backed by our human follow-up team: US & Canada-based pros who previously worked in real dental and ortho offices. They cover business hours, roughly 7 AM to 7 PM — including lunch, when your front desk can't answer — and they work every lead until it's a booked consult or a clear no.

No bots. No offshore call center reading a script. And no leads piling up in a dashboard you'll check on Friday.

Social ads FAQ

Do Facebook and Instagram ads still work for orthodontists?+

Yes — when they're run as direct-response campaigns, not brand fluff. Parents and adult patients aren't searching for braces every day, but they're scrolling every day, and a strong offer in front of the right household starts treatment conversations that would never have happened otherwise. It's the same playbook our founder, Dr. Tyler Coles, used to grow his own practice from 2 to 7 locations into Arizona's largest private orthodontic practice, with 80% of new patients coming from internet leads rather than dentist referrals.

What ad budget do I need for social media ads?+

Ad spend is separate from our management fee and paid by you directly to the platforms, so every dollar goes toward reaching patients. The right budget depends on your market size, competition, and how many new starts you want — a single-location practice in a smaller market needs far less than a multi-location group in a metro. On your strategy call we'll map your market and recommend a starting budget, then scale it as the ROI proves out.

Who makes the ad creative?+

We do. Hooks, images, video edits, ad copy, and offers are produced by our team and tested continuously — you don't need to become a content creator to run winning ads. If you can share real photos and clips from your practice, even better: authentic creative usually outperforms stock, and we'll tell you exactly what to capture.

How do leads from social ads get followed up?+

By real humans — a US and Canada-based follow-up team of people who previously worked in dental and orthodontic offices. They cover business hours, roughly 7 AM to 7 PM including lunch, respond to every lead in 20 minutes or less, work each lead until it's a yes or a clear no, and book consults directly in your own practice management software for your team.

What does social media marketing for orthodontists cost?+

Social ads management is included in the OrthoBoost Growth Plan starting at $2,000/month — alongside Google Ads management, SEO + AI optimization, and your website. Ad spend is paid separately to the platforms, there are no setup fees, and it's month-to-month after an initial 3-month commitment. You also get a free $10,000 website with the 3-month plan, backed by our 90-day ROI guarantee: if you're not profitable in 90 days, we work free until you are. Details on the pricing page.
